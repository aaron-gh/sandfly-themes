# SandFly sound themes

Sound themes for [SandFly](https://github.com/aaron-gh/SandFly).

## Themes

* **Bell** (`Bell.zip`): Glassy FM bells in fifths and octaves, with the same soft shimmer at the end of each sound.
* **Chip** (`Chip.zip`): Playful retro game sounds, round and warm, with a tiny soft echo after each one.
* **Desk** (`Desk.zip`): Small real-world sounds from one wooden desk, with no tune at all.
* **Dune** (`Dune.zip`): Sand grains, seeds and a rainstick over a low singing hum.
* **Felt** (`Felt.zip`): A felt piano under a blanket: soft struck notes, all below the voice.
* **Glow** (`Glow.zip`): Hummed tones that swell in and fade out, with no strike at all.
* **Kalimba** (`Kalimba.zip`): Warm plucked tines, D5 to D6. The home note D rings softly under every sound.
* **Kiln** (`Kiln.zip`): Clay pots played by hand, with breath from the pot's mouth, a tap on the shell, and a few embers.
* **Percussion** (`Percussion.zip`): Woodblock, clave, snap and soft clicks, with one soft home pitch under every hit.
* **Rainwater** (`Rainwater.zip`): Water drops layered with a soft glass-bowl ring and a little air. Each drop lifts in pitch as it rings.
* **Wind** (`Wind.zip`): Breaths and gusts with a light whistle for pitch and direction, all from the same breeze.

Bell, Chip, Desk, Dune, Felt, Glow, Kalimba, Kiln, Percussion, Rainwater and Wind are by Matthew Whitaker. They include control sounds, which are off until Control sounds and vibrations is turned on in the settings of the theme.

* **SandFly 0.2.1** (`sandfly-0.2.1.zip`): the sounds of SandFly 0.2.1, before SandFly 0.3.0 replaced them. As in 0.2.1, control sounds, screen on, row wraps, cursor moves and reading control changes have no sound. The circle menu, braille display connection and braille keyboard typing sounds are off. Vibrations added after 0.2.1 are off.

## Installing a theme

In SandFly, go to Settings > Sound and vibration > Sound themes, choose From a source under Install a theme, and choose a theme from this repository. From SandFly 0.6.1, this repository is in the list until it is removed. In earlier versions, or after removing it, add it:

    https://github.com/aaron-gh/sandfly-themes

To install one theme from a link, choose From a link under Install a theme and enter the link to its file, such as:

    https://github.com/aaron-gh/sandfly-themes/raw/master/Bell.zip

To go back to the current sounds, choose the SandFly theme in Sound themes.
